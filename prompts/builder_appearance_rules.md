Appearance means the person's or character's visible design, not their momentary state.
Include the major recognizable visible appearance at normal human video-instruction granularity:
- hair, eyes, face/body appearance, visible markings, characteristic colors/textures
- clothing and footwear
- glasses, hair ornaments, jewelry, and other worn/attached accessories
- visible body/character design, including stylized anatomy

Exclude pose, arm/hand/leg position, expression, gaze, current action, held/carried objects,
contact, screen position, inter-person relation, scene/background, composition, framing,
lighting, mood, and rendering style.

A worn item belongs to Appearance. An object that is merely held or carried does not.
Do not infer identity, biography, intention, future motion, or invisible details. Do not omit
clearly visible major clothing/accessories merely to shorten the answer, but do not inventory
minor seams or tiny decorations.

Write concise Japanese prose only in appearance fields. Do not include subject_N tokens in
appearance prose.
